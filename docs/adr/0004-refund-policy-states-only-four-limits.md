# The Refund Policy states only four limits

The public Refund Policy says only four things: refunds are available for 15 days counting the purchase day, with under 2 hours watched, under half the lessons completed, and one refund per student. It deliberately leaves out the finer rules: claimed team seats cannot be refunded, watching after asking still counts ([ADR-0001](0001-refund-eligibility-judged-at-two-moments.md)), and progress is kept after a refund ([ADR-0003](0003-one-refund-per-student-ever.md)). Spelling out every edge teaches people how to game the rules, and once detail is published it cannot be taken back. It also says nothing about how admins review refund requests.

## Considered Options

- **A complete policy that lists every rule.** Rejected: it trades a little transparency for a guide to misuse.

## Consequences

- Do not "complete" the page by adding the missing rules. They were left out on purpose.
- The link to the Refund Policy sits quietly among the site's other supporting links, on the homepage and on every course page, so it can be read before buying. It is not a banner or a call to action.

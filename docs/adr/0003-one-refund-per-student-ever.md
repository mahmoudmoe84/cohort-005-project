# One refund per student, ever

A student gets at most one refund across all of Cadence (only approved refund requests count; declined ones do not use it up). This follows Udacity's model. It closes both misuse loops with one rule that is simple to state publicly: buying, watching and getting a refund on the same course again and again, and doing the same thing from course to course. A refund ends the student's access to the course but keeps their progress, and they may buy the course again. That new purchase can never be refunded, because they have already had their one refund.

## Considered Options

- **Block buying the course again after a refund.** Rejected: it turns away a paying student, and the one-refund rule already makes buying again safe.
- **Only limit a second refund on the same course.** Rejected: it still allows a refund on every other course.
- **Delete progress when a refund is approved.** Rejected: it cannot be undone, it erases the record of what was watched before the refund, and keeping it means a student who buys again picks up where they left off.

## Consequences

- This is a public promise in the Refund Policy. Loosening it later is easy, but tightening it would break what students were told.
- After a refund, the course page shows the normal Buy button with one quiet line saying when the student was refunded. The "Request a refund" box never appears again for that student, on any course.
